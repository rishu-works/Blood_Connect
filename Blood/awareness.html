Know before you donate

Blood Donation Awareness

General information to encourage informed and responsible participation.

♡

Why blood donation matters

Donated blood and its components may be needed during surgeries, serious injuries, childbirth complications and treatment for some illnesses. Blood services rely on eligible voluntary donors.

✓

Eligibility and screening

Eligibility depends on factors such as age, health, weight, medicines and recent activities. A qualified blood-bank professional must assess donor suitability before donation.

＋

Before and after donation

Follow the blood centre's instructions, eat and drink as advised, answer screening questions honestly, and follow the staff's after-care guidance.

?

Frequently asked questions

Can everyone donate? No. Eligibility is assessed by trained staff.
Can this website confirm blood availability? No. It is an educational prototype and does not verify donor availability.

Medical note: This page provides general awareness only. Ask a licensed blood bank or healthcare professional for current eligibility rules and medical advice.
